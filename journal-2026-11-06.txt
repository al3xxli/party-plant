Plant Band - 6 November 2026

We replaced the monstera after discovering an ant infestation, switching to a trailing, variegated plant. Our interactions became stem bending (flex sensor), leaf pinching (FSR), pot tilting (MPU6050, replacing soil moisture), and stem touch (MPR121 with copper tape). We dropped the extra pot-touch interaction.

An Arduino UNO R3 sends readings over USB to a p5.js interface, animating the plant and making sound through the laptop. We calibrated the flex sensor around its naturally curled resting position.

The main difficulties were incorrect I2C wiring, unreliable touch detection, and changes initially living in the wrong project folder. Sharing A4/A5 worked once SDA/SCL were connected correctly. Automatic electrode calibration fixed copper touch and release.

All four interactions now produce sound. Playing together revealed a balance problem: the sounds overlap, but one can mask another. We strengthened the drum, raised the chime's register, and added individual volume controls. The revised mix still needs a group listening test.
